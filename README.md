# Awesome **Open Source** Alternatives

## Acknowledgements
This list was originally created by Deepti Chopra (@deeptivchopra), who started it in May 2023 as a simple, community-curated catalog of open-source alternatives to everyday SaaS products.
What began as a handful of entries has grown, through dozens of contributors and forks across the community, into one of the most widely referenced lists of its kind.
Every addition to this repository builds on that original idea and the openness with which it was shared, our thanks to Deepti for starting it.

Find below, my version of the list of **open-source alternatives** to everyday SaaS products.
